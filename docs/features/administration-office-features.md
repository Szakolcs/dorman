# Administration and Office Features

## Tenant Management

- Tenant list and detail views with student/payment status indicators
- Tenant registration and archiving
- Monthly billing  and fines overview

## Room and Capacity Management

- Room list and detail views with occupancy indicators
- Assignment and reassignment workflow for tenants
- Capacity validation and conflict handling

## Inventory Management

- Room-level and shared inventory tracking
- Availability and condition status fields
- Assignment history for accountable items

## Maintenance Management

- Ticket creation with severity and location
- Assignment and SLA target tracking
- Status pipeline from open to closed

## Operational Scheduling

- Staff job scheduler with date/time windows
- Conflict hints for overlapping assignments
- Calendar and list visualization modes

## News and Activity Publishing

- Draft and publish workflow for dorm news/articles
- Activity listing with organizer and capacity
- Visibility controls for tenant audience
